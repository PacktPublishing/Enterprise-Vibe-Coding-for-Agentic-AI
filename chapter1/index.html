The Five Levels of Engineering Velocity

1

Traditional Development

No meaningful AI integration. Engineering relies entirely on human throughput, manual processes, and conventional tooling.

2

AI-Assisted (Autocomplete Era)

Individual developers use AI for code completion and snippets. Adoption is ad hoc — no shared standards, no governance, no methodology.

3

Vibe Coding

Teams actively use AI for generation, but without discipline. Fast first drafts, growing hidden debt. Prompt strategies are personal, not institutional.

4

Structured Agentic Development

Emerging HVE practices. Primitives are being adopted — instructions, reusable prompts, defined agents. The RPI loop is forming. Governance is catching up.

5

Hypervelocity Engineering

Full HVE methodology. Primitives are codified, auditable, and version-controlled. Small teams operate at 10× capacity. Business value is measured continuously.
